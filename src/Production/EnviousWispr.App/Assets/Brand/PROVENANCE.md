- `EnviousWisprMark.svg` is byte-identical to `assets/readme/icon.svg` in the
  EnviousWispr macOS repository.
- `EnviousWisprAppIcon.png` is the 1024 px RGBA representation extracted from
  the authoritative macOS `AppIcon.icns`.
- `EnviousWispr.ico` is a Windows multi-resolution icon generated from that
  1024 px representation at 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 128, and 256 px.
